# Clinical Trial Study Design

## 1. Study Identification

* **ClinicalTrials.gov ID:** NCT02537470
* **Study acronym:** BRID
* **Study phase:** Phase II
* **Condition:** Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
* **Study type:** Interventional
* **Study status:** Completed
* **Actual enrollment:** 191 participants

## 2. Study Objective

The study evaluates the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of biphasic remogliflozin etabonate compared with placebo in adults with Type 2 diabetes mellitus.

## 3. Study Design

* Randomized
* Double-blind
* Parallel-group
* Placebo-controlled
* 12-week treatment period

## 4. Treatment Groups

Participants are assigned to:

* Placebo
* Biphasic remogliflozin etabonate

## 5. Primary Outcome

The primary efficacy outcome is HbA1c at Week 12.

## 6. Secondary Outcomes

The study evaluates changes in:

* HbA1c
* Fasting plasma glucose
* Insulin
* C-peptide
* Total cholesterol
* LDL cholesterol
* HDL cholesterol
* Serum triglycerides
* Body weight
* Waist circumference

These measurements are assessed at Weeks 4, 8, and 12.

## 7. Safety Assessments

Safety assessments include:

* Adverse events
* Vital signs
* ECGs
* Standard laboratory tests
* Urinary tract infections
* Genital fungal infections

## 8. Eligibility

The study includes adults aged 18–80 years with a clinical diagnosis of Type 2 diabetes mellitus.

One key inclusion criterion is an HbA1c between 7.0% and 10.5% at screening.

## 9. Data Used in This Portfolio Project

This project will not use identifiable participant-level data from the clinical trial.

The published study design will be used as the clinical and analytical foundation. Synthetic participant-level data will be generated for educational purposes.

The synthetic data will allow the project to demonstrate:

1. Relational database design
2. SQL querying
3. Data cleaning and quality checks
4. Clinical-trial data structures
5. CDISC SDTM concepts
6. ADaM concepts
7. Descriptive statistics
8. Statistical analysis
9. Clinical-trial tables, listings, and figures
10. Reproducible documentation and version control
